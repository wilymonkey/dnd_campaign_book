Campaign Book

  • Premise: The Sun no longer sets in the Duke's Gardens. Knights have been sent to investigate, yet none have returned. In your hand you hold a bounty that simply reads: *4000GP to any party that can end the curse of eternal daylight".
  • Expect: We will be navigating a hex-crawl sandbox, not every combat encounter can be won with a head on approach and the threat of character death is real. D&D 5E but with custom rules that make the game harder.

What do you need to do?

  • Create 2 level 3 characters.

House Rules

  • All characters use the standard array for ability scores: 15, 14, 13, 12, 10, 8. Use the Standard Array by Class for guidance.
  • CON is not added to HP. HP is rolled at the table. HP isn't recovered completely at full rest, but you can GRIT YOUR TEETH in combat to regain HP.
  • No bonus actions. All bonus actions are converted are either free or now cost an action.
  • We're using Vancian Magic of OD&D. See below for details.
  • You now die when: you are reduced to -10 HP or you fail a death saving throw. You lose 1 HP for every round you are unconscious.
  • Initiative is side based and is rolled as players vs DM.
  • No backgrounds. Write a short, 3-4 sentence backstory instead.

Vancian Magic

  • Spells are cast at their native level.
  • Casting involves 2 major steps:
    1. Memorisation: Choose the spells you'll memorise for the day.
    • Artificers, clerics, druids and paladins: Can memorise [ level + 2 ] spells. Spells need to be [ level / 2 ] or lower.
    • Bards, Sorcerors, Wizards, Warlocks: Can memorise [ level x 2 ] spells. Everytime you level, learn 3 spells of [ level / 2 ] or lower.
    1. Fire-and-Forget: Once a spell is cast, it is forgotten from the character's mind and cannot be cast again until it is re-memorized. If a Magic-User wants to cast "Magic Missile" three times in a day, they must dedicate three of their memorisation slots to "Magic Missile".
