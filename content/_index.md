# Campaign Book

- **Premise:** The Sun no longer sets in the Duke's Gardens. Knights have been
  sent to investigate, yet none have returned. In your hand you hold a bounty
  that simply reads: _"4000GP to any party that can end the curse of eternal
  daylight"_.
- **Expect:** We will be navigating a hex-crawl sandbox, not every combat
  encounter can be won with a head on approach and the threat of character death
  is real. D&D 5E but with custom rules that make the game _harder_.

### What do you need to do?

- [Create 2 level 3 characters.](https://5e.tools/book.html#xphb,2)

## House Rules

- All characters use the standard array for ability scores: 15, 14, 13, 12,
  10, 8. Use the
  [Standard Array by Class](https://5e.tools/book.html#xphb,2,assign%20ability%20scores,0)
  for guidance.
- CON is not added to HP. HP is rolled at the table. No hit dice.
- No bonus actions or reactions. All such actions are either free or now cost an
  action.
- We're using Vancian Magic of OD&D. See below for details.
- When you are knocked unconcious, roll on the lingering injuries table below.
- Initiative is side based and is rolled as players vs DM.
- No backgrounds. Write a short, 3-4 sentence backstory instead.

### Vancian Magic

- Spells are cast at their native level.
- Casting involves 2 major steps:
  1. **Memorisation:** Choose the spells you'll memorise for the day.
     - Artificers, clerics, druids and paladins: Can memorise \[ level + 2 \]
       spells. Spells need to be \[ level / 2 \] or lower.
     - Bards, Sorcerors, Wizards, Warlocks: Can memorise \[ level x 2 \] spells.
       Everytime you level, learn 3 spells of \[ level / 2 \] or lower.
  2. **Fire-and-Forget:** Once a spell is cast, it is forgotten from the
     character's mind and cannot be cast again until it is re-memorized. If a
     Magic-User wants to cast "Magic Missile" three times in a day, they must
     dedicate three of their memorisation slots to "Magic Missile".

### Lingering Injuries

**Recovery:** Long rest to remove an injury.

| d6 | Injury                                                         |
| -- | -------------------------------------------------------------- |
| 1  | **Exhausted:** Disadvantage on ability checks.                 |
| 2  | **Wrenched Joint:** Speed halved.                              |
| 3  | **Concussed:** Disadvantage on attack rolls and saving throws. |
| 4  | **Bruised and Battered:** Hit point maximum halved.            |
| 5  | **Slow:** Only take 1 action or movement during your turn.     |
| 6  | **Dazed:** Fail checks relying on sight or hearing.            |
